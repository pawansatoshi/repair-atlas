## Eligibility checkpoints

The official rules state that individuals must be at least 18 or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. Teams may contain up to five individuals. Certain countries and territories are excluded under the official rules.

Eligibility must be checked against the official rules immediately before submission.

## New-project requirement

The project must have been newly created during the Submission Period. Standard development tools, frameworks, libraries, starter templates, and AI coding assistants are permitted, but other pre-existing code/work incorporated into the submission must be disclosed as required by the rules.

RepairAtlas is a new repository created for this hackathon. Future commits should preserve a clear development history.

## Intellectual property

All incorporated third-party software, SDKs, APIs, data, templates, and assets must be used under applicable permissions/licenses.

No copyrighted music or unauthorized third-party material should appear in the demo video.

## Testing requirement

Before submission, provide access to a working project for judging/testing through a public demo or equivalent instructions. If authentication is required, provide appropriate testing credentials in the submission instructions without exposing secrets in the repository.

## Stage One gate

The project must clearly fit the agentic-memory theme and meaningfully use the required CockroachDB/AWS technologies.

## Stage Two strategy

The five judging criteria are equally weighted:

1. Agentic Memory Design
2. Technological Implementation
3. Real-World Impact
4. Product Readiness
5. Creativity & Originality

Detailed judge strategy is in [`judging-strategy.md`](judging-strategy.md).
